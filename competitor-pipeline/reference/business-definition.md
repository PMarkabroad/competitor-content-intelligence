# What Ark Abroad is — the relevance test

Source: https://www.arkabroad.com/ (read 2026-09-04). This file is the single
source of truth for "is this content relevant". Scripts read it rather than
carrying their own idea of the business, because they had drifted: the
pipeline had been treating Ark as an "AU/US/CA" business and generating
posts written for American and Canadian readers, which Ark has no audience
for.

## The audience — all four must be true

1. **Already in Australia.** Not planning to come, not deciding where to go.
2. **Holds valid Australian work rights.** The site says outright it is not
   for people who lack them. Visa attainment is therefore not our subject —
   our reader has already solved it.
3. **Working a survival job** — hospitality, retail, driving, warehouse,
   front desk — while wanting a corporate role.
4. **Internationally trained.** Overseas degree or overseas experience that
   the Australian market is discounting.

## The market

**Australia only.** "Built for the Australian market, not generic advice."
Every generated post is written for a reader in Australia. There is no US
audience and no Canadian audience.

US and Canadian competitors are still tracked, and that is deliberate — they
are the largest career-content accounts anywhere and their HOOK PATTERNS and
FORMATS transfer. What does not transfer is their subject matter and their
market. Borrow the shape, never the market.

## What we sell

1. Positioning & profile rebuild — resume to Australian standards, LinkedIn,
   career narrative
2. Proof-of-work & market signal — portfolio projects, role-specific
   credibility
3. Referral & outreach engine — direct hiring-manager outreach, recruiters
4. Interview conversion — behavioural and technical prep, 1:1 accountability

Outcome language: survival job to corporate role. 70+ verified placements,
$5M+ in offers, and the "14 months" wait we help people skip.

## Not relevant — do not tag as a hook, do not generate as a post

- **Visa, PR, sponsorship, immigration pathways.** Our reader already has
  work rights. This is the single most common drift, because competitors
  cover it constantly and it performs well for them.
- **Any other country's immigration** — H-1B, OPT, green card, Express
  Entry, LMIA, PGWP.
- **Content written for a US or Canadian reader**, including a US/CA
  employment market, salary bands, or job boards.
- **Getting INTO Australia**, choosing a country, or comparing countries.
- **Remote jobs, work-from-home, job listings, vacancy drops, "apply here".**
- **Nationality rankings** — "top 10 countries by", intake by nationality.
- **Regulated-profession registration** — medicine, nursing, law, teaching,
  engineering registration. Different problem, different buyer.
- **Backpacker / working-holiday / farm-work / 88-day content.**
- **Student recruitment or course selling.** We are not an education agent.
- **Generic career advice with no Australian specificity** — if a US coach
  could publish the same words unchanged, it is not ours.

## Relevant

Anything that moves an internationally-trained person already in Australia
from a survival job into a corporate one: Australian resume conventions,
how Australian hiring actually reads overseas experience, local-experience
objections, recruiter and hiring-manager behaviour in Australia, interview
performance, salary conversations in Australian bands, networking without a
local network, positioning a foreign degree, and the psychology of being
overlooked while overqualified.
