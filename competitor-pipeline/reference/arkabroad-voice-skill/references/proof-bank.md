Read before writing anything containing a statistic. **If a number isn't in this file, it doesn't ship.** Outcome claims in a migration-adjacent business attract complaints; every figure needs to be defensible.

Figures below are as stated by the founder in transcripts and need periodic re-verification. Marketing owns keeping this current — treat anything with a ⚠️ as needing a source before external use.

## Rules for using these

1. **Round down, never up.** "Over 100 placed" not "nearly 150."
2. **Attach a range where you have one.** Ranges read as real; round single numbers read as marketing.
3. **Never imply a guarantee.** "We've helped 100+ students land roles" is a track record. "We'll get you a role" is a promise nobody can make and it invites a complaint.
4. **Never state or imply anything about visa outcomes.** Ark Abroad is a career accelerator, not a migration agent. In Australia, immigration assistance is a regulated activity. Career coaching content must not drift into advice about visas, sponsorship eligibility, or migration pathways — refer out instead.
5. **Follower and view counts go stale fast.** Check the account before publishing, or use a soft floor ("a community of thousands").
6. **Client case studies need written permission** — name, employer, salary, and photo each separately. Salary especially: a client may be happy to be named but not to have their package published.
